## What the trend actually is

"Burning Bridges" is a song from Drake's album Iceman, released on May 15, 2026 with its music video. The scene everyone remakes comes near the end: a private-room party under candle-bulb chandeliers, the lead walking up to the camera with a silver cup, pointing at the lens, holding a flat hand over the eyes to scan the room, then raising the cup. From late September people started using AI to put someone else in that spot, and in early October it was everywhere. The hand over the eyes was already a meme of its own; here is [where "where she at" comes from](https://burningbridgestrend.org/where-she-at).

The joke is the contrast: the most "I made it" party you can picture, with the least likely person at the center.

## Three ways to make one

| Route | What you do | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Film it yourself | Friends, a cup, a phone and the trending sound | Free, and 100% you |
| Swap or prompt with a general AI video tool | Upload a clip or write the scene as a prompt, then retry until the cup and hands look right | Your own twist on the scene |
| One-photo template site | Upload one photo, download a finished clip | A finished clip without prompting or editing |

## Tools

Template sites (one photo in, the scene already set up):

- [Burning Bridges Trend](https://burningbridgestrend.org/) (ours): one photo of you or your pet, 15 seconds, landscape 16:9, an original beat and party sound but never the song, a brand-new scene with no music-video footage. Paid per video, no free tier.
- burning-bridges.com: one photo, pets allowed, 20 seconds, 16:9, silent output.
- burning-bridgesai.com: a lead photo plus up to three friends, 15 or 28 seconds, 16:9, no music.
- tryburningbridges.com: one photo, 10 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16, the site says the song is laid over the video.
- burningbridgesai.app: a lead photo plus up to three friends or pets, 16 or 28 seconds; the site says it swaps the performer in the trend clip and keeps its soundtrack.
- burningbridges.io: one photo, 15 seconds, delivered as a widescreen and a vertical file.

General AI video tools:

- Dreamina: has a prompt page for the trend; you write or adapt the prompt yourself.
- Higgsfield and Kling: can copy motion from a reference clip you upload.

## Photo habits that keep faces right

- One person or one pet, facing the camera, nothing over the face.
- Waist up for people: the cup, the point and the hand over the eyes all need arms.
- Even light, no sunglasses or caps; the eyes carry the whole bit.
- No beauty filter, and a recent photo.
- Pets work too: one animal, eye level, sharp face. More in this [guide to the Burning Bridges trend with your pet](https://burningbridgestrend.org/burning-bridges-ai-pet).

## Adding the song and the AI label

- Most one-photo tools give you a clip without the song. Add it in the app you post with: TikTok's Add sound, Instagram's Audio, YouTube Shorts' Add sound. Search "Burning Bridges" and slide it so the cup goes up on a strong beat.
- Switch on the AI-generated label. TikTok, Instagram and YouTube all ask creators to label realistic AI content.

## Consent and rights

- Use your own photo, a photo of an adult who said yes, or your pet. Celebrity and athlete photos are refused by most tools for good reason.
- Clips built on the original music video reuse footage and audio that belong to someone else; they are the easiest for platforms to mute or remove.
- None of the tools above is official. The song, the video and the artist's likeness belong to their owners.
